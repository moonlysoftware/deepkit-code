# Retained beta evidence

These are selected original development measurements supporting
[the benchmark guide](../../../docs/benchmarks.md), plus the beta audit receipts.
`manifest.json` maps retained originals to their former paths and hashes;
compressed originals retain their original decompressed bytes.

Paths and build identities inside historical reports describe the original
experiment. They are intentionally not rewritten to imply a current build.
The complete journals, transcripts and intermediate runs remain in the verified
external development archive and historical Git commits. They are not required
to build or use DKC.

The retained query sets include reviewed ripgrep inputs and explicitly marked
draft Laravel, Django and Hugo inputs. Diagnostic cohorts and previously seen
questions are not fresh holdouts. Failures and target misses remain visible.
